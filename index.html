Behold is a mystery game set in the stories of the Bible.

Each case is a passage: you study the pictures of its moments, work out who is who and what happened, and fill the blanks of the account in the game’s own words — then the reveal shows the text itself.

You don’t need to remember the story. Everything you need is in the picture.

    Case one

    Two altars on a mountain, a bull on each, and all Israel watching. What did the people answer, what did the fire burn, and what did the people do then?

  6. The vineyard

    Case two

    A man walks a vineyard as if it were his, and a prophet has come to meet him. Whose vineyard was it, and what became of its owner?

  7. The battle

    Case three

    A crowd of prophets tells a king to go to war, and one plain man is sent to prison. Then two kings ride into battle, and only one of them is dressed as a king. Who is the man in plain armor, and what became of his chariot?

  8. 2 Kings

  9. The room

    Case four

    A woman builds a small room on her roof for a holy man who often comes her way. Years later she rides hard to find him, and tells all who ask that everything is all right. Whose room is it, and what happened on its bed?

  10. The gift

    Case five

    A commander of Aram comes to Israel’s prophet with leprosy, and goes home clean. Then the prophet’s servant runs after the commander, with an answer for everyone who asks. Who sent him, and where had he been?

  11. The window

    Case six

    Years after a prophet’s word against a king, spoken in a vineyard, a commander is anointed in secret and sets out for Jezreel. Two kings ride out to meet him, and a queen watches from her window. On whom was the word kept, and how?

Playing on an iPhone?

Behold runs in Safari. Put it on your Home Screen before you start, and it opens like an app and keeps your progress.

  1. Open play.beholdgame.com in Safari.
  2. Tap ⋯ beside the address bar, then Share. No ⋯? Tap Share at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, leave Open as Web App on, and tap Add.

A Safari tab and the Home Screen keep separate progress, and Safari can clear a site’s saved progress after a while away.

Free, with nothing to buy

Behold has no accounts and no ads, and it collects nothing that says who you are. Your progress stays on your device, and the game never sends it anywhere. It counts how its cases are played, never who plays them. You can turn Counting off in About, at the foot of the cases page.
