How it plays

Look. Solve. Close the case.

Behold is a mystery game set in the stories of the Bible.

You don’t need to remember the story. Everything you need is in the picture.

  1. 1 · Look

    Tap what matters

    Each picture is a moment of the story. Tap anything that looks like it matters. Each word you find goes to Solve.

  2. 2 · Solve

    Put it together

    Name who is who, put the pictures in story order, and fill the account’s blanks with the words you found.

  3. 3 · Close the case

    Read what happened

    When it all fits, close the case and read the passage itself. Then the next case opens.

Stuck? Ask for a hint.

It shows you where to look, then exactly what to tap.

Start with the valley. It teaches the game.
